# FuzzyBit Library

These are papers I wrote over a span of fifteen years since 2011.
The first paper began as a study of the patent but then turned into a study of logic's roots - back to its origins.
As you can see, everything from modern to ancient and archeological reasoning is studied, everything from east to west to the heart of past.
Logic cannot be studied without philosophy so I studied that, too, and I found a new appreciation for Aristotle and mostly the Stoics.

Each of these papers are a study into philosophy - the study of reason.
But each paper is also a study into modern thinking.
The work, expense, time and energy I spent trying to make software became a quest to understand reason and thinking.
With the superiority of artificial intelligence over so much of science, technology, engineering and math it is bound to upend everything.
And the last fifteen years has come steaming forward to the present in the fuzzybit library.
